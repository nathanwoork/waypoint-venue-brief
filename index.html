Waypoint Venue Brief

Research and launch plan for an English-first venue on Tonberry that links casual players, roleplayers and raiders. Pick a tab to jump between the research, the venue assessment and the step-by-step launch plan.

Patch 7.5x · end of Dawntrail Evercold Jan 2027 Tonberry Standard Active players 7,639
Bottom line

Build a weekly connector night for English-speaking SEA players, opened before Evercold. Skip the big-budget nightclub model.

Unlimited gil removes the cost problem only. Venues close because of staff, regulars, housing luck and timing, and none of those are for sale.

  1. Tonberry is still Elemental's English hub, but it is one of eight similar-sized worlds on the smallest Japanese data center. The audience is real and modest.
  2. Casual players have the clearest unmet need. RP is thin on Elemental and works as programming. Raiders come for matchmaking and old-content nights.
  3. Switch 2 (August) and Evercold (January) bring new players who need an English landing spot. Open in mid-November to catch them.

Community reports · long-running

Two communities share one world

English speakers dominate the visible social layer: free companies, Party Finder, hunts and linkshells. Duty Finder skews Japanese, and Japanese players mostly run their own events. Players have long described the split as roughly even, with the English side louder.

Inference

The English crowd now leans Southeast Asian

Tonberry became the English home for SEA and Oceanic players before Materia (Oceania's own data center, 2022) existed. With Australia and New Zealand now served by Materia, the English base reads more GMT+8 than Australian.

Raid guides

Raiding culture splits by difficulty

Savage Party Finder on Elemental follows Japanese macros. Japanese players don't run Ultimates through Party Finder here, so English players set those strategies through their own Discords. Dancing Mad (Ultimate, patch 7.51) is the current prog target.

News

Social play took two hits

August 2024 travel limits on congested worlds made venue hopping harder and started a shakeout of the post-Endwalker venue boom. In August 2025 the Mare Synchronos mod, which much of the RP scene used to share custom looks, shut down after a legal inquiry.

Etiquette

Japanese norms set the tone

Party Finder listings marked J only are meant for Japanese speakers. Shouting for groups is less welcome on Japanese worlds than on NA ones. English players do best queuing with both J and E ticked and keeping social chat in English spaces.

News · plus inference

A newcomer wave is lined up

The Switch 2 version launched on 4 August 2026 and Evercold arrives in January 2027. Aegis, Carbuncle, Kujata and Typhon are Preferred and will take new characters, who can visit Tonberry freely within the data center.

Casual chatters

The largest English group, and the clearest gap.

Where they gather
GMT+8 international free companies and their Discords, FC estates, hunt trains, and Party Finder for maps, field operations and Cosmic Exploration.

English conversation outside instances, low commitment, somewhere to show off glamours and housing.

A fixed weekly place to meet people beyond their own FC.

Small and loyal. They make a venue memorable but can't fill it alone.

Where they gather
English RP linkshells and Discords, a few SFW venues such as Izakaya Himitsu (Tonberry, The Goblet, W12 P41), and FC houses. The game's RP centre stays on NA Crystal.
Rhythm
Scheduled nights of two to three hours that depend on regulars.
What they want
Consistency, immersion, low drama, clear rules. Cozy taverns over loud clubs.

Thin numbers on Elemental, the loss of synced custom looks after Mare, staff burnout.

A stage, and non-RP guests who might try it.

Raiders

Already organised. They come if a venue saves them time or offers something new.

Where they gather
Party Finder, statics, and English Discords for Elemental Ultimate pugs and raid macros. Some travel to Mana for Savage.
Rhythm
Locked raid nights and reclears. A burst at each new tier, then churn as statics break up.
What they want
Reliable statics, fill-ins, clears, and fresh challenges between tiers.
Friction
Static churn, J-only listings, and a long gap before Evercold's first tier.
What a venue gives them
A recruitment board and weekly synced old-content nights.
Venue fit: medium through throwback nights.

04 · Venue test

The venue idea, stress-tested

Unlimited gil is the premise. It solves the money side completely and leaves the hard parts untouched.

What gil buys

  • Any plot you win. Typical asking prices:
3–5MSmall plot
15–20MMedium plot
40–50MLarge plot
  • A professional designer and full furnishing.
  • Prizes, bounties, staff wages.
  • Relocation to a better ward later.

What gil can't buy

  • Regulars. Venues that opened on giveaways alone became the clones players now mock.
  • Staff who stay. A venue veteran reported wage bidding wars pushed running costs to over four times their original level.
  • A time zone that suits everyone, or a bridge across the language split.
  • Safety for bought gil. Real-money trading breaks the terms of service and Square Enix bans accounts in large waves.

A layout that links the three crowds

Working name "Waypoint" · large plot, three zones

Lounge for the casual crowd

The default room. Greeters say hello in English, and signs carry short Japanese lines so Japanese visitors feel welcome. FCs and linkshells pin recruitment notes, which makes your venue part of their recruiting.

Stage for roleplay

One light-RP night a week that casual guests can join without a character sheet, plus a monthly story arc for regulars. SFW and readable on a vanilla client.

The Board for raiders

Sign-ups for weekly synced throwback raids, a static matchmaking night at each new tier, and a wall of clears. Gil goes into bounties for groups formed here, not door prizes.

05 · Launch plan

From empty plot to opening night

Assumes you already hold a large plot on Tonberry. Jump to a part:

First stepsStaffDesignersRaiders and Party FinderDiscordWhere to meet people

First steps, starting this week

  1. Week 1

    Write a one-paragraph concept and house rules. Pick the name and a fixed night between 20:00 and 23:00 GMT+8. Set up the Discord skeleton, a cross-world linkshell and a simple carrd page.

  2. Week 1

    Recruit two officers you already trust. They cover nights you can't and set the culture.

  3. Weeks 1–2

    Brief and hire a designer. A large build takes up to four weeks, so start now.

  4. Weeks 2–4

    Run two or three throwback raid nights through Party Finder. They need no house, so the raid side grows while the venue is decorated.

  5. Weeks 2–4

    Partner with three to five English FCs on Tonberry and Kujata. Offer them a recruitment board in the lounge.

  6. Week 3

    Recruit the rest of the staff and run trial shifts.

  7. Weeks 4–5

    Soft-open for Discord members, then open publicly with a Party Finder listing, a Lodestone event and a Reddit post. Mid-November leaves time for newcomer nights before Evercold.

Recruiting staff for the first opening

Where to find them

  • English FCs on Tonberry and Kujata. Use the Lodestone Community Finder (Elemental, English) and message FC leaders. Their members staff a night, their FC gets lounge exposure.
  • Existing English venues on Tonberry, such as Izakaya Himitsu. Experienced staff often work several venues. Partner, don't poach.
  • SEA player groups. A 2021 Lowyat thread pointed Malaysian players to Tonberry and to a Facebook group for SEA and Oceanic players on the Japanese data center. Check it is still active.
  • r/ffxivrecruitment with an [Elemental] tag.
  • Venue-owner Discords. They exist but lean NA/EU.

Opening-night roster

Host / manager ×1Greeters ×2Bartender ×1RP lead ×1Board host ×1Moderator ×1Bard or DJ (optional)

Recruit in this order: two trusted officers, then experienced venue staff, then the wider pool. Use a trial shift before confirming anyone.

Pay steadily and modestly. One published rate is about 300k gil per night plus tips. Outbidding other venues brings staff who leave for the next offer.

Hiring a designer

The Elemental catch

Most designers with public price lists play on NA or EU. Materia is the only data center all regions can visit, so an NA or EU character can't reach Tonberry. The designer needs a character on Tonberry itself, because some housing and all FC functions are unavailable while visiting from another world.

  • Personal estate: add them as a tenant in the estate's shared settings.
  • FC estate: invite them to the FC with furnishing permissions.
  • Send furniture gil to their Tonberry character; unused budget usually comes back.

Where to look and what to ask

  • Designers' carrd pages (see the Budget tab), Elemental Discords, and the Japanese housing community on X under #FF14ハウジング.
  • Ask for venue builds in their portfolio, deposit terms (50% upfront is common), turnaround, and whether unused budget is returned.
  • Ask how they place items. Some use third-party layout plugins, which sit outside the terms of service.

Raiders who want older expansions

Throwback nights

Weekly synced (Minimum Item Level) old raids. Syncademy proved the model on the Light data center, growing to about 1,900 Discord members. No English equivalent turned up on Elemental.

  • Rotate one expansion a week: Coils, Alexander, Omega, Eden, Pandæmonium, plus old Extreme trials.
  • Once a month, a learning party for an older Ultimate. Elemental's Ultimate PF is already English-led.
  • Hooks at the venue: a clears wall, and an expansion passport stamped per raid, with a glamour or gil prize for a full one.

Listing it in Party Finder

  • Post the duty in its own category, on the Data Center tab.
  • Objective: Practice for prog, Duty Completion for clears. Condition: Duty Incomplete on first-timer nights.
  • Tick E and J and add 英語メイン・日本語OK ("mainly English, Japanese OK").
  • Start every listing with the same tag so people learn it.
  • Put the venue social night under the Other category.
  • Never offer runs for sale (banned since 2021) and keep wording clean; players reported suspensions over a suggestive RP venue listing in 2022.

Settings that matter on day one

  • Turn on Community features and Onboarding so members pick roles: Casual / RP / Raider, Tank / Healer / DPS, time zone, EN / JP.
  • Raid-Helper for sign-ups with role slots. Discord's built-in AutoMod for moderation.
  • Rules: SFW server-wide, gil-only economy, no real-money trading, no mod requirements, how OOC disputes are handled.
  • Two moderators covering different hours. Mirror it with an in-game cross-world linkshell.
  • Start with these channels and add more only when one gets busy.

Where to meet people

In game

  • Hunt trains, field operations and treasure maps, where chat flows naturally.
  • Your own throwback Party Finder nights, the best recruiting tool you have.
  • Other English venues and FC events. Befriend the owners.
  • Shout chat sparingly; shout spam is unwelcome on Japanese worlds.

Online

  • Lodestone Community Finder (list your linkshell) and Event & Party Recruitment (filter Elemental and English).
  • Elemental Ultimate Pugs and Elemental DC Raid Macros Discords.
  • r/ffxivrecruitment, the SEA Facebook group, the Lowyat forum.
  • X and Bluesky for screenshots of the build.
