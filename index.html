Bottom line

Build a weekly connector night for English-speaking SEA players, opened before Evercold. Skip the big-budget nightclub model.

A 200M gil budget covers the build, not the hard parts. Venues close because of staff, regulars, housing luck and timing, and none of those are for sale.

  1. Tonberry is still Elemental's English hub, but it is one of eight similar-sized worlds. From 13 October 2026 Japan's four data centers share matchmaking and Party Finder, so a venue listing reaches a region of about 262k active players. The English-friendly audience is real and modest.
  2. Casual players have the clearest unmet need. RP is thin next to NA Crystal and works as programming.
  3. Switch 2 (August) and Evercold (January) bring new players who need an English landing spot. Open before Evercold. With a plot lottery and a six-week showcase build, an early-to-mid December opening is realistic.

Inference

A bigger audience, a smaller English share

A Party Finder listing for the venue is now visible to the whole Japan region, which makes the reachable audience much larger than Elemental alone. The English-speaking share of that pool is not measured by the census used here, so keep E and J ticked and plan for a Japanese-majority crowd.

Community reports · long-running

Two communities share one world

English speakers dominate the visible social layer: free companies, Party Finder, hunts and linkshells. Duty Finder skews Japanese, and Japanese players mostly run their own events. Players have long described the split as roughly even, with the English side louder.

Inference

The English crowd now leans Southeast Asian

Tonberry became the English home for SEA and Oceanic players before Materia (Oceania's own data center, 2022) existed. With Australia and New Zealand now served by Materia, the English base reads more GMT+8 than Australian.

Raid guides

Raiding culture splits by difficulty

Savage Party Finder on Elemental follows Japanese macros. Japanese players don't run Ultimates through Party Finder here, so English players set those strategies through their own Discords. Dancing Mad (Ultimate, patch 7.51) is the current prog target.

News

Social play took two hits

August 2024 travel limits on congested worlds made venue hopping harder and started a shakeout of the post-Endwalker venue boom. In August 2025 the Mare Synchronos mod, which much of the RP scene used to share custom looks, shut down after a legal inquiry.

Etiquette

Japanese norms set the tone

Party Finder listings marked J only are meant for Japanese speakers. Shouting for groups is less welcome on Japanese worlds than on NA ones. English players do best queuing with both J and E ticked and keeping social chat in English spaces.

News · plus inference

A newcomer wave is lined up

The Switch 2 version launched on 4 August 2026 and Evercold arrives in January 2027. Aegis, Carbuncle, Kujata and Typhon are Preferred and will take new characters, who can visit Tonberry freely within the data center.

04 · Venue test

The venue idea, stress-tested

A 200M gil budget is the premise. It funds the plot and a showcase build, and leaves the hard parts untouched.

What gil buys

  • Any plot you win. Typical asking prices:
3–5MSmall plot
15–20MMedium plot
40–80MLarge plot
  • A professional designer and full furnishing.
  • Prizes, bounties, staff wages.
  • Relocation to a better ward later.

What gil can't buy

  • Regulars. Venues that opened on giveaways alone became the clones players now mock.
  • Staff who stay. A venue veteran reported wage bidding wars pushed running costs to over four times their original level.
  • A time zone that suits everyone, or a bridge across the language split.
  • Safety for bought gil. Real-money trading breaks the terms of service and Square Enix bans accounts in large waves.

Sign-ups for weekly synced throwback raids, a static matchmaking night at each new tier, and a wall of clears. Gil goes into bounties for groups formed here, not door prizes. On hold: the raiders crowd was removed from the demographic, so decide whether this zone stays before the designer brief.

05 · Launch plan

From empty plot to opening night

Assumes you win a large plot on Tonberry. The Budget tab covers its cost and how to bid. Jump to a part:

First stepsStaffDesignersRaiders and Party FinderDiscordWhere to meet people

First steps, starting this week

  1. Week 1

    Write a one-paragraph concept and house rules. Pick the name and a fixed night between 20:00 and 23:00 GMT+8. Set up the Discord skeleton, a cross-world linkshell and a simple carrd page.

  2. Week 1

    Recruit two officers you already trust. They cover nights you can't and set the culture.

  3. Weeks 1–2

    Shortlist large plots and enter the housing lottery (see the Budget tab). If the first entries lose, the deposit is returned and the next 9-day cycle starts, so allow extra time.

  4. Weeks 1–2

    Shortlist designers and agree a quote. A showcase build is more complex than a standard large house and can take up to six weeks, so the design starts as soon as the plot is won.

  5. Weeks 3–8

    Build with the designer, funded in stages, with weekly progress screenshots. Partner with three to five English FCs on Tonberry and Kujata and offer them a recruitment board in the lounge.

  6. Weeks 7–8

    Recruit the rest of the staff and run trial shifts.

  7. Weeks 9–10

    Soft-open for Discord members, then open publicly with a Party Finder listing, a Lodestone event and a Reddit post. An opening in early to mid December still lands before Evercold in January, with less time for newcomer nights than a November opening.

Recruiting staff for the first opening

Where to find them

  • English FCs on Tonberry and Kujata. Use the Lodestone Community Finder (Elemental, English) and message FC leaders. Their members staff a night, their FC gets lounge exposure.
  • Existing English venues on Tonberry, such as Izakaya Himitsu. Experienced staff often work several venues. Partner, don't poach.
  • SEA player groups. A 2021 Lowyat thread pointed Malaysian players to Tonberry and to a Facebook group for SEA and Oceanic players on the Japanese data center. Check it is still active.
  • r/ffxivrecruitment with an [Elemental] tag.
  • Venue-owner Discords. They exist but lean NA/EU.

Opening-night roster

Host / manager ×1Greeters ×2Bartender ×1RP lead ×1Board host ×1Moderator ×1Bard or DJ (optional)

Recruit in this order: two trusted officers, then experienced venue staff, then the wider pool. Use a trial shift before confirming anyone.

Pay steadily and modestly. One published rate is about 300k gil per night plus tips. Outbidding other venues brings staff who leave for the next offer.

Hiring a designer

The Elemental catch

Most designers with public price lists play on NA or EU. Materia is the only data center all regions can visit, so an NA or EU character can't reach Tonberry. The designer needs a character on Tonberry itself, because some housing and all FC functions are unavailable while visiting from another world.

  • Personal estate: add them as a tenant in the estate's shared settings.
  • FC estate: invite them to the FC with furnishing permissions.
  • Send furniture gil to their Tonberry character; unused budget usually comes back.

Where to look and what to ask

  • Designers' carrd pages (see the Budget tab), Elemental Discords, and the Japanese housing community on X under #FF14ハウジング.
  • Ask for venue builds in their portfolio, deposit terms (50% upfront is common), turnaround, and whether unused budget is returned.
  • Ask how they place items. Some use third-party layout plugins, which sit outside the terms of service.

Raiders who want older expansions

On hold. The raiders crowd was removed from the demographic after review. Treat this section as background until it is decided whether the Board and throwback nights stay.

Throwback nights

Weekly synced (Minimum Item Level) old raids. Syncademy proved the model on the Light data center, growing to about 1,900 Discord members. No English equivalent turned up on Elemental.

  • Rotate one expansion a week: Coils, Alexander, Omega, Eden, Pandæmonium, plus old Extreme trials.
  • Once a month, a learning party for an older Ultimate. Elemental's Ultimate PF is already English-led.
  • Hooks at the venue: a clears wall, and an expansion passport stamped per raid, with a glamour or gil prize for a full one.

Listing it in Party Finder

  • Post the duty in its own category, on the Data Center tab.
  • Objective: Practice for prog, Duty Completion for clears. Condition: Duty Incomplete on first-timer nights.
  • Tick E and J and add 英語メイン・日本語OK ("mainly English, Japanese OK").
  • Start every listing with the same tag so people learn it.
  • Put the venue social night under the Other category.
  • Never offer runs for sale (banned since 2021) and keep wording clean; players reported suspensions over a suggestive RP venue listing in 2022.

The large plot: cost and how to win one

There is no auction. Unoccupied plots are sold through a housing lottery, so the "bid" is a lottery entry with a deposit. Price ranges below come from a third-party guide, so confirm the exact price on the placard in game.

What it costs

30–50MPersonal large plot
50–80MFree Company large plot
55MPlanned in the forecast
  • The plan uses a Free Company house near the low end of its range, because staff and the designer need decorating permissions through the FC.
  • Entry needs a deposit of 50% of the plot price, about 27.5M at the planned price. It counts toward the price if you win and is refunded if you do not.
  • Each 10M added to the plot price lowers the week-16 balance by 10M. At 80M the balance falls to about 35M. At 40M it rises to about 75M.
  • The plot is mostly a sunk cost. Selling a house back returns only part of the price.

How to enter the lottery

  1. Get the requirements ready: a character with a job at level 50 and a Grand Company rank of Second Lieutenant or higher, plus a Free Company led by someone on the team.
  2. Open the lottery at a plot placard in the housing district and pick the plot, ward and size.
  3. Pay the deposit. Entries stay open for the first five days of each 9-day cycle.
  4. Wait for the results on days six to nine. A win asks you to pay the rest. A loss refunds the deposit.
  5. Enter again next cycle. You can enter several plots at once.

Rules, ranks and timings can change. Confirm each step in game before paying.

Raising the odds

  • Enter every cycle and enter several plots at once, spread across wards.
  • Newer wards have more plots, and Empyreum has more than Lavender Beds, so they are easier to win than the old popular wards.
  • Stay flexible on district. A showcase build can be designed to suit the plot's shape and neighbours.
  • Keep the deposit funds ready in one character's inventory, so no cycle is missed.

Risks and what not to do

  • Winning is not guaranteed. The forecast assumes a first-cycle win in weeks 1–2. Each lost cycle costs about nine days and delays everything after it.
  • Housing is tied to a home world. The Japan region change does not move the plot, so the plot stays on Tonberry.
  • Never pay real money for a plot or an FC. Real-money trading breaks the terms of service and gets accounts banned.

Why the plan sits above the price lists

  • The listed fees are for standard large interiors. A layered, custom showcase with an exterior, three themed zones and detailed lighting is more work, so the plan uses 32M for the designer, with a range of 25–45M. This is an assumption to replace with real quotes.

Design language

One shared look for the in-game house, the Discord, the booking site and every screenshot. Everything below is a proposal for Summer to edit and confirm; the colours and fonts are the ones already used on this page, so the site and the venue can match from day one. Finished pieces feed "Design the brand kit" and "Brief and manage the designer" above.

Concept: the crossroads

A waypoint is the marker you plant so a group can find each other. The venue is the same idea: a lit stop on the road where three kinds of traveller meet. Every design decision should say "you can find your people here".

  • Readable first. Most people will meet Waypoint through a screenshot, so the exterior and each room must read clearly in one image.
  • One doorway, three directions. The entrance lobby points to Lounge, Stage room and the Board with obvious signage.
  • Vanilla only. Furniture and glamour obtainable in-game, no mod-gated looks, so every guest sees the same room.
  • SFW and warm. Dim, inviting light; nothing that needs an age gate.

Voice and naming

  • Tone: plain, friendly English. Short sentences, no gatekeeping slang.
  • Greeting: "Welcome in. First time here? The Lounge is on your left."
  • Party Finder tag: [WPT] plus the night's activity, e.g. "[WPT] Throwback raid night, new players welcome".
  • Zone names: the Lounge, the Stage room, the Board. Keep them as written everywhere.
  • Never: real-money talk, selling runs in listings, or unconfirmed claims about opening dates.

The Lounge

Warm lantern light, mixed seating in small clusters so strangers can drift into conversation. Mint accents. Newcomer desk and FC recruitment boards by the door.

The Stage room

Raised stage, curtains, a clear sightline from every seat. Rose accents. Space for a bard, a DJ or a small RP scene without blocking the walkway.

The Board

Noticeboard wall for throwback signups and a clears wall for trophies. Coral accents. Reads like a guild hall: functional, a little competitive, still friendly.

What the numbers say

  • The plot, designer and furniture cost 147M of the 184.5M, about 80%, and are paid in weeks 1–8.
  • The balance crosses the 100M buffer line in week 6 and ends at 60.3M, so about 39.7M of the buffer is used by week 16.
  • Once open, running costs are 5.0M a week against 2.8M of income, so the balance drifts down about 2.2M a week. At that rate the 60.3M lasts roughly 23 more weeks before reaching the 10M floor.
  • The plan assumes the plot is won on the first lottery cycle. Each lost cycle delays the build by about nine days.

Levers to narrow the gap

  • A cheaper plot: every 10M saved lifts the week-16 balance by 10M.
  • Crafting part of the furniture: 10% less is about 6M back.
  • Five staff instead of six saves 0.5M a week. Performers monthly instead of every other week saves 0.25M a week.
  • Break-even needs about 2.2M a week more income, roughly 314k gil a day on top of the 400k. Resources lists ways to earn it.
  • Never top up with bought gil. Real-money trading gets accounts banned.

What to look for on each visit

  • Who staffs the room: hosts, bartenders, bards or DJs. Note names, roles and who seems stretched thin.
  • The night and the gap: which nights are already busy, and which are empty. Waypoint's 20:00–23:00 GMT+8 slot should not clash with a friendly venue.
  • What guests enjoy: contests, raffles, music, games. Borrow ideas for Kurome's idea bank.
  • Whether they are English-first: those are your likely partners and cross-promotion targets.
Summer

Closing the deal and convincing people to join

These are suggested approaches, not guarantees. The aim is that people say yes because the offer is clear, fair and low-risk, not because they were pressured.

  1. Step 1 · be a regular

    Show up, tip, join in. People hire people they already like. Keep a short note of each person you meet and what they enjoy.

  2. Step 2 · lead with their problem

    Performers want steady paid work and credit. Hosts want a rota that respects their time. Raiders want a place to show clears. Say how Waypoint solves that, not how great Waypoint is.

  3. Step 3 · make the offer concrete

    Role, night, hours, pay, and who to ask. Waypoint's planned wage is 500k per staff shift, paid in gil on the night and logged by Kurome. Clear numbers build trust faster than enthusiasm.

  4. Step 4 · lower the risk

    Offer a paid trial shift or a single guest set first. Make it additional to their current venue, not a replacement, so they never have to choose on day one.

  5. Step 5 · be a good neighbour

    Never recruit inside another venue during its opening hours, ask in a private message, and take no for an answer. Offer cross-promotion in return: a shout-out on the Board, a guest slot for their staff.

  6. Step 6 · follow up and keep them

    After the trial, confirm the role and schedule in writing, ask for feedback, and review at the weekly sync. People stay where they feel heard and paid on time.

Check the performer budget

The forecast books a performer about every other week at roughly 1M a booking. For a rough benchmark, one Crystal and Primal performance troupe advertises 450k an hour for a lead bard, 250k an hour for each extra bard and 300k per dancer. That is a different data center and not a Tonberry quote, but one lead-bard hour at 450k is already 45% of a 1M booking, and a two-hour set would use almost all of it. Get real quotes before committing, and consider shorter sets or a monthly performer if costs run high.

Ways to offset spending

After opening, running costs are 5.0M a week against 2.8M of income, a gap of about 2.2M a week. That is roughly 314k gil a day, nearly double the 400k a day now coming in. No single option closes it, but a mix of the options below can narrow it. All of them use gil only, in line with the venue rules.

Option 1

Craft for the market board

  • Look up what sells quickly on Tonberry with Universalis, then craft what has a healthy margin over material cost.
  • Use Teamcraft to list materials and plan the crafting chain.
  • Outside guides rate crafting and market-board selling as the strongest option for experienced crafters. Expect a startup cost for good gear.
  • Venue bonus: craft housing furniture and consumables yourself where possible, instead of buying them. Every 10% saved on the 60M furniture budget is 6M kept.
Option 2

Gather and sell materials

  • Farm high-value nodes and sell the materials directly. Low skill requirement, steady output.
  • Time it to patches. New materials sell at a premium for the first days, so Evercold in January 2027 is the best window and lines up with the planned newcomer night.
  • Crafters also need your materials, which supports Option 1.
Option 3

Treasure maps and other solo grind

  • Treasure maps are a low-effort, fairly steady earner that can run while chatting in Discord.
  • Third-party guides estimate roughly 1–2M gil an hour for maps against 3–5M for expert crafting. These are rough figures and depend on the week and world.
  • Good as a filler when crafting stock is waiting to sell.
Option 4

Raid assistance for gil

  • Offer clear help or guidance to players stuck on content, with gil as a thank-you or agreed fee, arranged through your Discord or free company.
  • Do not advertise it in Party Finder. Square Enix prohibits advertising runs for currency there, and Waypoint's own rule says the same.
  • Gil only. Never take or ask for real money.
  • Keep it clearly separate from the venue so guests do not feel they are being charged.

Spend less without cutting quality

  • Five staff instead of six saves 0.5M a week.
  • Performers monthly instead of every other week saves 0.25M a week.
  • Win the plot near the low end of its range. Every 10M saved is 10M kept.
  • Craft or source part of the furniture list instead of buying it all.
  • Pay some bounties and prizes in crafted glamour, furniture or passport stamps instead of gil.
  • Reuse seasonal decor and rotate it instead of buying new each time.

Bring a little gil in

  • A voluntary tip jar for guests, clearly optional.
  • Sponsored nights from partner free companies that want a themed evening.
  • Paid private bookings of the house for other groups, if Summer agrees the schedule allows it.
  • Review monthly: if the balance trends toward the 10M floor, pull one lever from each list. The 100M buffer line is the early warning.
